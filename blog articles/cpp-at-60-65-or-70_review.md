2. **Promises or implies results:** Needs a second look: CTA: "We build Retirement Income Plans that optimize your income for a tax efficient draw down." "Optimize" could be read as a promised outcome. Possible softer version: "that aim to make your income draw down as tax efficient as possible."
3. **Unrepresentative statistics or conclusions without assumptions:** Clear. The table and break-even example are labelled hypothetical, assumptions are stated, and it notes most people receive less than the maximum.
4. **Opinion or forecast not labelled:** Clear. "Our view is that…" and the attributed quotes are labelled. "Savings can deplete quickly; a larger CPP cheque does not" is a general statement of how indexing works, not a forecast.
5. **Risks and downsides left out:** Clear. Both sides are presented (shorter vs longer retirement, health, debt, DB pension).
6. **Detrimental to the public or Investia:** Clear.
7. **Conflicts with legislation or guidance:** Clear.
8. **Inconsistent with other published information:** Clear, as far as existing site content goes.
9. **Implies regulator approval:** Clear.

## E. What not to say (for social and client conversations)

| Avoid | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Always wait until 70 for CPP." | "Deferring CPP raises your monthly amount, but whether that pays off depends on your health, other income and how long your retirement lasts." |
| "Taking CPP at 60 is a mistake." | "Starting at 60 permanently reduces your pension by 36%; for some people that trade-off still makes sense." |
| "You'll get $17,040 a year if you wait." | "In our hypothetical example of someone entitled to $1,000 a month at 65, starting at 70 would pay $1,420 a month. Your own amount depends on your contribution history." |
