# Your website rebuild — the plan in plain English

Hi Heidi,

Your site has served you well, but after 12 years on WordPress it's carrying real weight: two competing page-building tools loaded at once, a dozen plugins we don't need, two compliance items that slipped through the cracks, and a page still crediting FundEX as your dealer (Investia absorbed them in 2018). Rather than patch, I'd like to rebuild — and use a cleaner, simpler setup that costs less to run and is easier to keep current.

## What I'm recommending

Move off WordPress onto a modern static-site setup. Your visitors will see a faster, cleaner, more professional site. Your disclaimers, your brand, your content, your phone numbers — all of it stays. Under the hood, the site becomes a set of plain-text files we can update as confidently as we'd update a Word document.

## Why this is better for your business

**Lower cost.** Hosting drops from ~$40-50/month to around $15/month. No more plugin license renewals.

**Dramatically faster.** Your site will load in under a second. That matters for Google rankings and for how prospects experience you in their first 10 seconds.

**Compliance-friendly.** Every change to a disclaimer or a page is logged and auditable — we can show Investia exactly what's on the site and exactly what changed. No more surprises.

**No maintenance treadmill.** WordPress requires constant updates (core, theme, 13 plugins). Skip one and you expose yourself to malware. The new setup has nothing to update.

**Built to last.** Platforms like Meta and WordPress come and go. The new approach is vendor-neutral — if we ever want to move, everything is portable.

## What changes for you

The biggest change: **you won't log in and edit pages visually anymore.** Instead, when you want a copy change or a new blog post, you send it to me, I ship it within a day, and you review. It's the same review flow we already use for your other marketing — just applied to the website.

If that's a dealbreaker, there's a version of this setup where you *do* get a simple editing interface. Say the word and I'll price that option. But for most of my clients, the "send it to me, I'll ship it" workflow works better in practice than logging in to WordPress ever did.

## What stays exactly the same

Your brand (plum, cream, Playfair Display, Open Sans). Your phone numbers. Your address. Every testimonial. The Investia disclaimer wording. The iA Financial Group privacy policy. Your team bios. Every blog post worth keeping. Your videos. Your calculators and downloads.

Want these fixed on the live site now, before the rebuild? I can do that this week — takes about an hour. Your call.
